# 🐾 Betina and Quesadilla: A Garden Adventure

A little story about two inseparable pugs, a sunny garden, and a few butterflies that are just a little too quick to catch. 🦋

**Betina and Quesadilla** is a short animated story created with Python and Pygame, as a special little gift for Valbb.

Because sometimes the best adventures aren't about catching something — they're about having someone beside you while you try. ♡

---

## 🌸 The Story

One sunny morning, Betina and Quesadilla head out to explore the garden.

Everything is peaceful until a golden butterfly flies right in front of their noses. Soon, more butterflies appear, and the two little pugs cannot resist the temptation to chase them!

They run, jump, and stumble across the grass, doing their very best to catch their colorful visitors. But the butterflies always seem to fly just a little higher than their tiny paws can reach.

Eventually, tired and out of breath, Betina and Quesadilla decide to rest together on the grass.

And then, something wonderful happens: the butterflies come back and gently land on their little noses.

That's when they discover that the best part was never catching the butterflies. It was enjoying the adventure together.

**The End.** ♡

---

## ✨ Features

- **Two adorable pug characters:** Betina, the black pug, and Quesadilla, the fawn-colored pug.
- **A colorful garden:** A sunny sky, rolling green hills, trees, flowers, grass, and a white fence.
- **Animated butterflies:** Four colorful butterflies with fluttering wings and playful movement.
- **Expressive characters:** Walking, running, jumping, blinking, panting, wagging tails, and adorable head movements.
- **Character dialogue:** Little speech bubbles bring Betina and Quesadilla's personalities to life.
- **Story captions:** Spanish narration guides the viewer through every moment of their adventure.
- **A heartwarming ending:** The pugs rest together as butterflies gently land on their noses.
- **Seamless story loop:** The 56-second animation restarts automatically.

## 🚀 How to Run

### 1. Clone the repository

```bash
git clone https://github.com/laetg404/betina-and-quesadilla.git
cd betina-and-quesadilla
```

### 2. Install the dependency

```bash
pip install pygame
```

### 3. Run the animation

```bash
python main.py
```

If your Python script has a different filename, replace `main.py` with its actual name.
